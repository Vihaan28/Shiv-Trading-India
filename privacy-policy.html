Privacy policy

How we handle the information you send us through this website.

Please have this reviewed before launch. This is a plain-language starting point written to match how the site actually works. It is not legal advice, and it should be checked against your obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 before you publish it.

Who we are

Shiv Trading India Private Limited operates this website. Our address is Bhagirath Palace, Chandni Chowk, New Delhi 110006, India. You can reach us by email at info@shivtradingindia.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into a form on this site. That is:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your delivery city, if you give it
  • The products you selected and the quantities you entered
  • Anything you write in the message or requirement box

We do not ask for payment details anywhere on this website, and we do not collect them.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry: to prepare a quotation, confirm availability, and follow up on your requirement. We do not sell your details to anyone, and we do not add you to a marketing list without asking.

Where it goes

This website is hosted on Netlify, and form submissions are stored in Netlify's form dashboard, which our team accesses to read your enquiry. Netlify may process this data outside India. We keep enquiries only for as long as they are useful for the business relationship.

What is stored in your browser

Your quote request list is stored in your own browser's local storage so it survives while you browse. It stays on your device and is never sent to us until you submit the form. Clearing your browser data clears it.

This site does not run advertising trackers.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@shivtradingindia.com and we will action it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page. This version was last reviewed when the website was published.
