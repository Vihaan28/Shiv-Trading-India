Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Shiv Trading India Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through shivtradingindia.com, why we collect it, where it is stored, and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

Shiv Trading India Private Limited operates this website and is the data controller for the information described below. Our registered place of business is Bhagirath Palace, Chandni Chowk, New Delhi 110006, India. You can reach us by email at stcshiv@gmail.com or by phone at +91 9650202197.

What information we collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us — through the quote request form, the contact form, or a WhatsApp/email/phone enquiry initiated from this site. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your company name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery location / city
  • The products and quantities you select for a quote
  • Anything you write in a message or requirement box

We do not ask for, and do not collect, payment card details or other financial account information anywhere on this website. We also do not use advertising trackers or sell data to third parties.

Why we collect it

We use the information above only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Prepare and send quotations
  • Understand your delivery requirements and confirm availability
  • Communicate with you as a prospective or existing customer about the enquiry you raised

We do not use this information for automated decision-making or profiling, and we will not add you to a marketing list without your consent.

Where it goes

Depending on how you contact us, your information may be stored in one or more of the following places:

  • Our internal enquiry records / customer database
  • Our email inbox, if your enquiry is emailed to us or forwarded there
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us on WhatsApp
  • Netlify's form-submission dashboard, which powers the "Request a quote" and "Contact" forms on this site and which our staff access to read and action enquiries

These service providers may process or store data outside India in the course of providing their service to us. We only share your information with people and services directly involved in responding to your enquiry — we do not sell or rent it to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry and quotation records for as long as they remain useful to the business relationship — typically for the duration of any ongoing discussion or order, and afterwards for a reasonable period to handle follow-up questions, repeat orders, warranty or accounts queries, and to meet our own legal and tax record-keeping obligations. Information that is no longer needed for these purposes is deleted or anonymised.

Who may have access

Access to enquiry and customer information is limited to Shiv Trading India staff who need it to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, or fulfil an order, and to the service providers named above (Netlify, and, where you choose to use them, email and WhatsApp) strictly for operating those channels on our behalf. We do not permit any other third party to access this information.

What is stored in your browser

The list of products you add to a quote request is stored in your own browser's local storage so it is remembered while you browse. It stays on your device and is never sent to us until you actually submit the request form. Clearing your browser data clears it. This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us:

  • What personal information we hold about you
  • To correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • To delete your information, where we are not required to keep it for a legitimate business or legal reason
  • To stop using your information for a particular purpose

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have any other question about how your data is handled, email us at stcshiv@gmail.com or call +91 9650202197. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for business-to-business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the way we handle enquiries changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date above.
